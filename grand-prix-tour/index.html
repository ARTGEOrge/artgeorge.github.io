How to play

Every race is a point-to-point road race between two towns. Start from the grid when the five red lights go out, pass the checkpoints and cross the finish line in the top three to unlock the next location.

  • W ↑
  • Throttle
  • S ↓
  • Brake (hold when stopped to reverse)
  • A D ← →
  • Steer
  • Space
  • Handbrake — kicks the rear round in hairpins
  • Shift E
  • Open DRS inside a green DRS zone (+25 km/h, closes when you brake)
  • C
  • Chase / cockpit camera
  • R
  • Reset onto the road
  • Esc P
  • Pause

Tuck in behind a rival for a slipstream tow. Barriers scrub speed, grass and gravel slow you right down. Phones and tablets get on-screen pedals.
