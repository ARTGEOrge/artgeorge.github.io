🎵 Antra desktop music library builder

Build a tidy local music library

Antra turns links from Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, Qobuz and Deezer into a local library, tagged with title, artist, album, artwork, genre and lyrics, and filed into Artist / Album folders that work with Navidrome, Jellyfin and Plex.

Formats: FLAC · ALAC · AAC · MP3
No Python. No setup. One app.

Quick start
  1. Launch Antra and pick your music library folder
  2. Choose your preferred output format
  3. Paste a link
  4. Press Add to Library
