Thirty-three years of kart racing

Kart Games — 16 playable versions

One browser game for every Mario Kart release, each built around the mechanic that game actually introduced. No install, no internet — open a file and drive.

Keyboard for all of them; mouse for Home Circuit and the VR range. Sound is on — it unlocks on your first key press (browser rule), and M mutes. Keep kart-gfx.js and kart-audio.js in this folder — every game loads them for sprites, effects and synthesised sound.
